---
title: gdtest.toml
description: gdtest's settings, in gdtest.toml or in gdsuite.toml's [test] section.
---

gdtest reads its settings from the `[test]` section of `gdsuite.toml`, or
from `gdtest.toml` when `gdsuite.toml` has no `[test]` section. The keys are
the same; profiles move from `[profile.ci]` to `[test.profile.ci]`.

| gdtest.toml | gdsuite.toml |
|---|---|
| `exclude = ["test/helpers/**"]` | `[test]`<br/>`exclude = ["test/helpers/**"]` |
| `[profile.ci]`<br/>`fail_fast = true` | `[test.profile.ci]`<br/>`fail_fast = true` |

gdtest reads the same file whether it runs through `gdsuite test`, from a
terminal, or from the editor panel. Keep its settings in one of the two
files: with a `[test]` section in `gdsuite.toml`, `gdtest.toml` is ignored,
with a warning.

See [gdtest's configuration reference](https://github.com/q-rault/gdtest#configuration)
for every key.
