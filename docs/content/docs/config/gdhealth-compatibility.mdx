---
title: gdhealth.toml
description: gdhealth's settings, in gdhealth.toml or in gdsuite.toml's [health] section.
---

gdhealth reads its settings from the `[health]` section of `gdsuite.toml`, or
from `gdhealth.toml` when `gdsuite.toml` has no `[health]` section. Each
section of `gdhealth.toml` goes under `[health]`:

| gdhealth.toml | gdsuite.toml |
|---|---|
| `[groups]` | `[health.groups]` |
| `[thresholds]` | `[health.thresholds]` |
| `[discovery]` | `[health.discovery]` |

Keep its settings in one of the two files: with a `[health]` section in
`gdsuite.toml`, `gdhealth.toml` is ignored, with a warning. Unlike
`gdhealth.toml`, the `[health]` section rejects unknown keys, so a section
written for gdsuite 0.1 is never taken for gdhealth's settings.

See [gdhealth's configuration reference](https://github.com/q-rault/gdhealth#configuration)
for every key.
